# The Data Swamp

## The Rotting Fen

Downstream of the Delta Crossing, the river loses its way and spreads into the Data Swamp: a sprawling fen of black water, rotting reeds and drowned paper. The swamp is made of walled-away records. Books, ledgers and letters that someone took from the realm and sealed up have sunk here and rotted into mud, and the mud is still faintly legible if you look closely. The water runs every way at once, and the swamp grows larger every year as more records are hoarded.

## Creatures of the Swamp

The swamp is dangerous. Swamp rats, fat and bristling, gnaw through anything with a label on it and travel in packs. Orphans drift over the water at dusk, scraps of lost records that lead travellers off the causeway into sucking mud. In the deeper channels a river wraith rises from the older currents to drag the unwary down. The bestiary records their strengths; travellers should go armed, and in daylight if they can.

## The Causeway

A single raised causeway of old stone crosses the swamp from the Delta's downstream landing towards the far side. Leave it, and the mud will have your boots, then your knees, then you. Wooden markers line the causeway, some newer than others, and it is unwise to trust the new ones.

## Warden Grisle

Halfway across, where the causeway narrows to a gate of black timber, Warden Grisle keeps the road to Silo's Keep. He is a heavy, scarred man in a grey coat, with a squad of Sealing Wardens at his back. He lets nobody pass who cannot satisfy him. Some say he can be fought, some say he can be bought, and a few say he can be talked round, for there is something he fears more than any traveller.

## The Keep Beyond

From the gate, on a clear day, you can see a grey fortress rising out of the far side of the swamp, its windows bricked shut.
