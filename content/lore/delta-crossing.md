# The Delta Crossing

## The River

At the Delta Crossing the river runs wide, cold and strange. Its waters move in visible layers, each current carrying the river as it was on a different day. Look closely and you may glimpse yesterday's leaves, last week's ice or a boat that sank a year ago, all drifting in their own currents side by side.

## The Ferry and the Old Bridge

A flat-bottomed ferry on a rope carries travellers across, worked by the Sweeper for two gold pieces a crossing. Upstream lie the ruins of the old stone bridge, which fell when the river changed course long ago. Brave or foolish travellers try to cross on its broken piers, a hard Might check in good weather.

## The Sweeper's Currents

The Sweeper reads the river the way a clerk reads a record. To those he trusts, he will pole the ferry out into an older current and let them watch the river as it was on another day: a drowned village, a summer flood, or the night the waters first turned back on themselves. He asks nothing for it but patience and honesty.

## Dangers

Orphans swarm over the water at dusk. River wraiths rise from the older currents after dark, especially on nights when the Delta runs backwards. Wading across is possible at the shallows in daylight, a normal Might check, but anyone who fails is pulled into an older current and comes out soaked, bruised and briefly unsure what day it is.

## Roads Onward

The ferry's far bank joins the road to the Unity Archive, half a day's walk upriver. A muddy track leads back to Lakehouse Village and the Medallion Inn. The Sweeper also keeps a second boat for the long run downstream, into the fens where the river spreads into marsh, but he takes no passengers on it except "those who carry what the river is owed".
