# The Spark Forge

## The Tower of Furnaces

The Spark Forge rises from a ridge north of the Archive, a tower of black brick and copper chimneys where Sparkia's mages learn to kindle. Rings of apprentices work around a single great flame at the tower's heart, each tending a share of the same fire. The air smells of hot iron and rain, and the whole tower hums like a struck bell.

## Forgemaster Ignatia Vale

Forgemaster Ignatia Vale runs the Forge with soot on her sleeves and a temper that burns out as quickly as it flares. She is broad-shouldered and grey-eyed, and she judges people by what they do with power once they have it. She keeps the Forge Seal on a chain about her neck and has refused it to princes.

## The Trial of Kindling

Anyone who asks for the Forge Seal must pass the Trial of Kindling. The candidate is given a single spark and told to split it across many hands: to pass it from apprentice to apprentice around the ring so that every forge lights at once, without letting it gutter or flare. The trial tests nerve, wits and the ability to coordinate others. A candidate who tries to keep the spark to themselves, or who hoards the glory, fails at once. Ignatia gives the Forge Seal only to those who share the spark rather than hoard it.

## The Darker Fire

The apprentices whisper that the Forge once made a different kind of fire, many years ago: a slow, smothering flame that did not warm but sealed, made to an order from someone the Forgemaster will not name. Ignatia does not speak of it. Those who ask gently, and who have earned her respect, sometimes see her look away towards the swamp.

## Visitors and Danger

The Forge is safe for honest visitors, but sparks are not toys. Careless hands can cause burns that a Might check will not shrug off, and an apprentice's flame lost in the middle of the trial means starting again.
