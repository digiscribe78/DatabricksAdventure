# The Medallion Inn

## The Building

The Medallion Inn is a three-storey coaching inn at the heart of Lakehouse Village. Its rooms are named for their comfort: the Bronze rooms in the draughty attic cost one gold piece a night, the Silver rooms on the first floor cost three, and the Gold suite with its own fireplace costs ten. Travellers say a night in Gold is worth every coin, and a night in Bronze is worth a story.

## The Common Room

The common room is low-beamed, smoky and always busy. A great hearth burns at one end, and a long oak bar runs along the other, where Marta Quill pours ale, cider and her famous blackcurrant cordial. A notice board by the door carries requests for help, lost-and-found notes and the occasional wanted poster.

## The Cellar

Beneath the inn is a cellar of barrels and old stonework that is older than the village itself. Marta keeps it locked. Regulars claim that on still nights you can hear water running somewhere below the floor.

## Regulars and Rumours

Old Tam the fisherman insists the Delta ran uphill three nights past. Bertram Keyes, a merchant in mud-spattered velvet, is stranded because his barge stuck when the river stopped flowing. Everyone agrees on one thing: since the Unity Archive sealed its doors, odd things have happened along the river.

## Prices

Ale costs a copper, a hot meal two coppers, and a stable for a horse one gold piece a night. Marta gives discounts to anyone who helps with trouble in the village.
