# The Kingdom of Sparkia: A Traveller's Primer

## The Realm

Sparkia is a kingdom of rivers, records and restless magic. Its people say that everything worth knowing is written down somewhere, and that everything written down is worth fighting over. The crown sits in distant Photon, a city of white towers that few villagers have seen, and the day-to-day running of the realm falls to stewards, guilds and the keepers of the great libraries.

## Lakehouse Village

Lakehouse Village sits where the river Delta widens into a slow, silver lake. It is a market town of timber halls and slate roofs, built on old stone footings that nobody remembers laying. Travellers come for the Medallion Inn, the ferry at the Delta Crossing and the road to the Unity Archive.

## The Delta

The Delta is no ordinary river. Its waters run in layers, each current carrying the river as it was on a different day. Fishermen swear they have hauled up yesterday's catch twice. Lately the Delta has misbehaved: on some nights it runs backwards, and on others it stops altogether.

## The Unity Archive

Upriver stands the Unity Archive, the great library that keeps the realm's records and decides who may read them. Every book there has a keeper, and every reader needs a writ. The Archive has sealed its doors in recent days, and no writ, bribe or prayer has opened them.

## Magic in Sparkia

Sparkia's mages call their craft "kindling". A spell is a spark, and a strong mage can split one spark across many hands so that a dozen apprentices finish a working in the time it takes one master. Mages train at the Spark Forge, far beyond the Delta.

## Rumours of Lord Silo

In taverns, people whisper of Lord Silo, a lord who hoards knowledge behind walls and lets nothing out. Some say he is a myth told to frighten scribes. Others say the Data Swamp in the east grows larger every year, and that is his doing.

## Customs and Coin

Sparkians pay in gold pieces and keep their word in writing. It is rude to read another person's letters, and a crime to burn a book. Travellers who know a good story are always welcome at the hearth.
