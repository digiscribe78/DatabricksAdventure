# The Streaming Falls

## The Curtain That Never Stops

West of the Archive, a great river drops over a cliff in a curtain of water that has never once paused in recorded history. The Streaming Falls fill the valley with mist and a roar like distant applause. Travellers who stand in the spray report the strangest thing about the place: the water seems to remember.

## Echoes in the Mist

In the mist, shapes form and replay what travellers once did. A woman sees herself, years younger, refusing a beggar. A soldier watches his own hand pass a forged order. The falls show echoes of choices, never of chance: not what happened to you, but what you chose. Some visitors weep, some laugh, and a few leave in a hurry. Scholars argue over whether the falls show only the past, or also choices made in other lives and other journeys.

## The Echo

The keeper of the falls is the Echo, a presence in the spray with no face of its own. The Echo speaks only in a visitor's own words: it repeats things you have said, sometimes things you said long ago and have forgotten, and lets you hear how they sound. It has never been known to lie, and it cannot be argued with, because every argument comes back in the arguer's own voice.

## The Falls Seal

The Echo holds the Falls Seal, a disc of clear stone that is always wet. It gives the seal to those who honestly face what the falls show them: who look at one of their own choices and own it, without excuses and without pretending. Lying to the Echo is pointless, since it only hands your words back to you. Silence earns nothing either.

## Visiting the Falls

The path down to the pool is slippery, and the spray soaks everything. Travellers should leave fire and paper well wrapped, and expect to stay longer than they meant to.
