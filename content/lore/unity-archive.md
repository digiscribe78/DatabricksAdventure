# The Unity Archive

## The Great Library

The Unity Archive crowns a hill of grey stone half a day upriver from the Delta Crossing. It keeps the records of the whole Kingdom of Sparkia: charters, maps, ledgers, songs and the private letters of kings. Every book has a keeper, and every reader needs a writ, a sealed paper that names what they may read and why. The Archive's motto is carved above the doors: *Known to those who should know.*

## The Bronze Doors and the Wards

The great bronze doors answer only to proper writs. Old wards are woven into their metal, and they are famous for their stubbornness: they cannot be flattered, bribed or fooled. Forged writs crumble to ash in the hand. Lies spoken at the doors echo back in the speaker's own voice. Travellers who try clever "magic words" find the wards simply do not hear them. The doors have been sealed shut for a fortnight, and not even the Archive's own keepers have come or gone.

## Keeper Aldric

Keeper Aldric, senior keeper for thirty years, is still inside. Those who knock and call his name may hear his voice through the bronze: tired, careful, and very much alive. He says he is holding the vault closed against someone who wants what lies within, and he will only speak plainly to someone he believes is a friend of the realm.

## The Vault and the Three Seals

Deep beneath the reading rooms lies the vault, where the Archive keeps the books too important to lend. Its door was built long ago by the realm's three great houses, and it opens only when all three seals are pressed into its lock together: the Forge Seal of the Spark Forge, the Falls Seal of the Streaming Falls and the Mines Seal of the Photon Mines. Each house gives its seal only to those who prove themselves.

## The Courtyard Roads and the Undercroft

From the Archive's courtyard, three old roads lead out to the three great houses. Beneath the building lies the undercroft, a maze of cold cellars where water runs under the Archive from somewhere far off. Keepers say the old builders knew of hidden ways in and out, though the maps were lost long ago.
