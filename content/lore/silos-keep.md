# Silo's Keep

## The Walled Fortress

Silo's Keep rises out of the far edge of the Data Swamp, a fortress built not of stone but of books. Its walls are volumes stacked and mortared together, its windows bricked shut with ledgers, its gates barred with bound charters. Everything the realm has lost seems to have come here to be walled away. The air is dry and silent, and the only sound is the faint scratching of pages settling.

## The Walled Corridors

Inside, the corridors are walled with bricked books from floor to ceiling, and many passages end in walls that were doorways once. Silenced Archivists, sentries bound into old tomes, patrol the halls and turn back anyone who has no business there. Torches burn without warmth. Travellers report that the corridors seem to rearrange themselves towards the centre, as if the Keep itself wants to bring visitors to its master.

## Lord Silo

At the heart of the Keep, Lord Silo sits among his walls. He is tall, gaunt and grey, dressed in the faded robes of a scholar rather than a lord, and he looks more weary than wicked. He is utterly certain that he is right: that knowledge left free will always be misused, and that walling it away is the only kindness left. He does not shout. He explains. He has been explaining to himself for a very long time.

## The Study and the Letters

Off the great hall lies a small study, the only room in the Keep with an open window. On its desk are bundles of old letters, tied with faded ribbon and read so often that their folds have worn through. Silo keeps them close and does not let his wardens touch them. Whoever wrote them, they matter to him more than anything else in the Keep.

## Facing the Lord of the Walls

Those who reach Silo will find he can be fought, out-argued or reached, but rarely all three. He is no fool. Visitors who come with proof, allies or understanding fare far better than those who come with only a sword.
