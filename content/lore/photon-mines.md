# The Photon Mines

## Galleries of Light

East of the Archive, the hills are riddled with the Photon Mines, where miners dig ore that shines like trapped light. The deeper galleries glow faintly blue, and lanterns are hardly needed. Photon ore powers the lamps of half the realm and makes the fastest-burning forges in Sparkia, which is why the mines are worked day and night.

## Shifting Tunnels and Chalk

The mines are unstable. After every collapse the tunnels shift, and yesterday's map is today's dead end. Miners survive by one rule: chalk every turn. Every junction is marked with a miner's sign and an arrow, and the oldest galleries are covered in thousands of chalk marks layered over each other. A lost miner finds the way out by following their own chalk back, turn by turn, to the last place they knew. Travellers are given a stick of chalk at the entrance and told to use it.

## Shiftmaster Odo Brack

Shiftmaster Odo Brack runs the mines. He is short, broad and loud, with forearms like mine props, and he cares about nothing in the world as much as his crew. He holds the Mines Seal, a disc of photon ore set in iron, and he will not trade it for gold. He has been known to trade it for a debt of honour.

## The Trapped Crew

Since the last great collapse, a crew of Odo's miners has been trapped deep in the lower galleries. Their tapping can still be heard through the rock. Odo has been digging towards them for days, but the tunnels keep shifting, and he needs someone small, brave or clever enough to find a way through. He would give anything to the one who brings his crew home.

## Strangers in the Deep

The miners report strangers in the deep galleries, men in grey who are not digging for ore at all. They seem to be tunnelling west, towards the old foundations under the hills, and they do not answer when hailed.
