# Bestiary and Notable Folk

## Marta Quill, Innkeeper

The keeper of the Medallion Inn. Wiry, iron-grey and sharp-eyed, Marta knows everyone's business and sells it by the half-pint. She is fond of honest travellers, ruthless with cheats, and quietly worried about her friend Keeper Aldric of the Unity Archive. She will trade a rumour for a good story or a fair coin.

## Keeper Aldric

The senior keeper of the Unity Archive, thirty years in the post and not the panicking sort. Three nights ago he sent his apprentice running to the inn in a terrible state, then barred the Archive's doors from the inside. Nobody has heard from him since.

## The Sweeper

The ferryman of the Delta Crossing, a tall figure in a salt-stained coat who never gives a name. The Sweeper keeps the crossing clear of old wreckage and older ghosts, and says that anything left too long in the river should be swept away. He charges two gold pieces a crossing and will not take more.

## Orphans

Small, pale lights that drift over the water at dusk: scraps of lost records that have come loose from their books. Orphans are harmless alone, but they gather in swarms and lead travellers astray. Hit points 4, attack +1, defence 10, damage a four-sided die.

## River Wraith

A drowned shape that rises from the Delta's older currents and drags the unwary down into yesterday. River wraiths fear lantern light and running feet. Hit points 12, attack +3, defence 12, damage a six-sided die plus 1.

## Swamp Rats

Fat, bristling rats from the Data Swamp that gnaw through anything with a label on it. They travel in packs of three or four. Hit points 5, attack +2, defence 11, damage a four-sided die.
