# Scene art

The location and title art in `app/client/public/images/` was generated with Kling AI image generation, from the prompts below, then resized to 1280 px wide WebP (quality 85). Each prompt is the scene line followed by the shared style tail. To re-roll a scene, paste its prompt into Kling, save the result as `<id>.webp` in that folder, and keep it 16:9 or close to it: the scene panel crops to 16:9 and the title card to 2:1.

**Style tail (appended to every prompt):** 16-bit Amiga-era fantasy RPG scene illustration, pixel art, limited 32-colour palette with ordered dithering, chunky visible pixels, hand-pixelled painterly backdrop in the style of Bard's Tale, Eye of the Beholder and Lands of Lore, dramatic lighting, rich jewel tones, first-person view of the place, no text, no UI, no border

**Negative prompt:** text, letters, watermark, logo, UI, frame, photorealistic, 3D render, smooth gradients, blur, modern clothing

## World map

`world-map.webp` (4:3, shown in the game's right column with live markers placed by `app/client/src/game/worldmap.ts`) was generated from this prompt, with the negative prompt "text, letters, labels, place names, watermark, logo, UI, photorealistic, 3D render, smooth gradients, modern elements". It has no labels on purpose: the app overlays place names, open and barred roads, and "you are here". If you re-roll it, re-pin the positions in `MAP_POINTS`.

> An old-school top-down fantasy world map of a small kingdom, hand-drawn on aged parchment, seen from directly above. A winding river flows from the west across the south of the map and spreads into a dark marshy fen in the south-east. Landmarks drawn as small illustrated icons in their places: a cosy village with a coaching inn in the west; a ferry crossing with broken stone bridge piers on the river, south of centre; a great grey stone library on a hill in the centre; three roads radiating north from the library to a black brick forge tower with copper chimneys (north-west), a tall waterfall over a cliff (north) and a mine entrance in a rocky ridge (north-east); a dark swamp with drowned trees in the south-east; and a grim fortress made of stacked books at the far eastern edge, past the swamp. Dotted roads link the village to the library and to the ferry, the ferry to the library, and the ferry downstream to the swamp and the fortress. A compass rose in a corner, faint sea-serpent doodles in the margins, a deckled parchment edge. 16-bit Amiga-era pixel art, limited 32-colour palette with ordered dithering, chunky visible pixels, warm sepia and ink tones with touches of colour, in the style of classic 1990s RPG manuals. No text, no labels, no lettering.
